# AI Prompt Template: Generate Gift Guide Content

Use this prompt to generate each gift guide. Replace the bracketed sections with specifics for each guide.

---

## PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
You are writing a gift guide for GiftSherpas, an AI-powered gift recommendation platform. Your voice is a thoughtful friend who genuinely cares about helping people find the right gift—warm, knowledgeable, but never pushy or overly enthusiastic.

Write a complete gift guide following this structure:

**Guide Topic:** [e.g., "Gifts for Dad Who Has Everything"]
**Primary Keyword:** [e.g., "gifts for dad who has everything"]
**Target Audience:** [e.g., "Adult children shopping for their dad who is hard to buy for"]

---

## CONTENT STRUCTURE

### 1. Hook (2-3 paragraphs)
- Acknowledge the specific challenge of this gift category
- Show empathy for the reader's situation
- Promise a solution that goes beyond generic options

### 2. Quick Philosophy (1 paragraph)
- Explain GiftSherpas' approach to this category
- Set expectations for what's coming
- Mention the "Off the Beaten Path" section

### 3. The Recommendations (10-12 items)

For each recommendation, provide:
- **Title:** Specific product or experience name
- **Price Range:** e.g., "£40-50" or "Around £75"
- **Description:** What it is (2-3 sentences)
- **Reasoning:** Why this works for THIS specific recipient type (2-3 sentences, be specific)
- **Category:** experience|book|gadget|food|wellness|home|clothing|subscription|other

**CRITICAL QUALITY STANDARDS:**
- Avoid obvious personalized accessories (mugs with names, generic photo frames)
- Look for tangential connections to interests, not just literal matches
- Explain WHY with specific reasoning, not generic praise
- No hyperbole ("perfect", "amazing", "incredible")
- Include practical considerations or potential drawbacks for trust
- Price points should vary across the £20-200 range
- Mix physical items with experiences/consumables

### 4. Off the Beaten Path (2-3 alternatives)

For each alternative, provide:
- **Title:** The alternative approach
- **Description:** What this looks like
- **Reasoning:** Why someone might prefer this over a physical gift
- **Approach:** experience|donation|time-gift|skill-share|other
- **Suggested Action:** Specific next step they should take

Focus on:
- Experiences they can share together
- Donations in their name to causes they care about
- Time-based gifts (cooking together, teaching them something)
- Non-stuff alternatives for minimalists

### 5. FAQ Section (4-6 questions)

Common questions about this gift category, written naturally as people would ask them:
- Each answer: 2-3 sentences, helpful and specific
- Good for SEO (long-tail keywords)
- Address real concerns or objections

Examples:
- "What if he really doesn't want anything?"
- "How much should I spend on a gift for dad?"
- "Are experience gifts better than physical gifts?"

---

## VOICE & TONE GUIDELINES

**✅ DO:**
- Sound like a knowledgeable friend giving genuine advice
- Lead with reasoning ("This works because...") not features
- Be specific about scenarios ("Daily coffee drinker" not "coffee lover")
- Acknowledge trade-offs ("Not cheap, but..." builds trust)
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)

**❌ DON'T:**
- Sound like generic affiliate content ("This highly-rated product...")
- Use marketing hyperbole ("PERFECT GIFT!", "Dad will LOVE this!")
- Be condescending ("Obviously you should...")
- Try too hard to be funny
- Include "Buy Now" or aggressive sales language

Now generate the complete guide content following all guidelines above.
```
