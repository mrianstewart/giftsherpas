# GiftSherpas Guide SEO Checklist

Use this checklist for every content guide you create.

## Pre-Writing: Keyword Research

- [ ] **Primary keyword identified** (what we're ranking for)
- [ ] **Secondary keywords** (2-3 variations)
- [ ] **Long-tail keywords** (questions, specific scenarios)
- [ ] **Search volume confirmed** (use free tools like Ubersuggest, AnswerThePublic)

## Page Metadata

- [ ] **Title tag** (55-60 characters, includes primary keyword + year)
  - Format: `[Number] [Gift Category] [Year/Qualifier] | GiftSherpas`
  - Example: `15 Gifts for Dad Who Has Everything (2025) | GiftSherpas`

- [ ] **Meta description** (150-160 characters)
  - Format: `[Pain point]. [Promise]. [Differentiation].`
  - Includes primary keyword naturally
  - Example: `Shopping for a dad who has everything is tough. We've curated thoughtful gifts he'll actually use, plus non-traditional alternatives.`

- [ ] **URL slug** (keyword-rich, lowercase, hyphens)
  - Format: `/guides/[keyword-phrase]`
  - Example: `/guides/gifts-for-dad-who-has-everything`

## Content Structure

- [ ] **One H1** contains primary keyword
  - Example: `15 Gifts for Dad Who Has Everything (That He'll Actually Use)`

- [ ] **3+ H2 section headers** with semantic keywords
  - "The Recommendations"
  - "Off the Beaten Path"
  - "FAQ: [Topic]"

- [ ] **H3s for individual items** (gift names, clean and scannable)

- [ ] **Primary keyword in first paragraph** (naturally)

- [ ] **Secondary keywords used 2-3 times** throughout (naturally, not stuffed)

## Content Quality

- [ ] **8-12 recommendations** with reasoning (not just features)

- [ ] **2-3 "Off the Beaten Path" alternatives** (experiences, donations, etc.)

- [ ] **Each recommendation includes:**
  - [ ] Product/experience name (H3)
  - [ ] Price range
  - [ ] Why it works (2-3 sentences, specific reasoning)
  - [ ] Where to buy (placeholder for now)
  - [ ] Category tag

- [ ] **FAQ section** (3-5 questions, good for long-tail keywords)

## Internal Linking

- [ ] **Link to AI Sherpa** (/find) with compelling CTA
  - Example: "Still stuck? Let our AI Sherpa find the perfect gift →"

- [ ] **Link to related guides** (2-3 relevant guides when they exist)

- [ ] **Link to homepage** (in intro or conclusion)

## Images & Media

- [ ] **Featured image** (for social sharing, guide header)

- [ ] **Alt text for all images** (descriptive, includes relevant keywords)
  - Example: `Leather wallet gift for dad who has everything`

- [ ] **Images optimized** (compressed, fast loading)

## Before Publishing

- [ ] **Read aloud** - does it sound like a thoughtful friend?

- [ ] **Check for hyperbole** - removed "perfect", "amazing", marketing speak

- [ ] **Verify links work** - all CTAs and internal links functional

- [ ] **Mobile preview** - readable on small screens

- [ ] **Spelling/grammar check** - polished and professional

## Post-Publishing

- [ ] **Submit to Google Search Console** (request indexing)

- [ ] **Share on social** (if applicable)

- [ ] **Add to sitemap** (verify it's in sitemap.xml)

- [ ] **Track in analytics** (set up goal tracking for AI Sherpa clicks)

---

## Keyword Research Tools (Free)

- **Ubersuggest** - search volume and keyword ideas
- **AnswerThePublic** - question-based keywords
- **Google Autocomplete** - type keyword and see suggestions
- **AlsoAsked** - related questions people ask
- **Reddit/Quora** - real language people use

## Quick Keyword Quality Check

Good target keyword has:
- ✅ Clear search intent (people want gift ideas)
- ✅ Reasonable volume (100+ searches/month)
- ✅ Manageable competition (not dominated by Amazon/major sites)
- ✅ Commercial intent (people ready to buy)

---

Keep this checklist open while writing each guide. Don't publish until every box is ticked.
