# 17 Gifts for Dad Who Has Everything (2026)

Shopping for a dad who has everything feels impossible. He's sorted—financially comfortable, set in his routines, and if he wanted something, he'd have bought it himself by now. The classic dad gifts (ties, tools, gadgets) feel tired, and you're left staring at Amazon wondering what could possibly make an impact.

Here's the thing: the best gifts for dads who have everything aren't about filling a gap in his stuff collection. They're about tangential connections to what he already loves, consumables that elevate his existing routines, or experiences that create new memories rather than clutter. Think less "here's a thing you don't have" and more "here's something that makes what you already do better."

We've curated recommendations that go beyond the obvious, plus some "Off the Beaten Path" alternatives for when physical gifts just aren't the answer. Or, if you'd prefer a personalised approach, <Link href="/find" className="text-emerald-600 hover:text-emerald-700 underline">try our AI gift finder</Link> to get recommendations tailored to your dad's specific interests.

## Our Approach

The recommendations below focus on three angles: upgrading things he uses daily (but wouldn't splurge on himself), introducing him to adjacent interests he hasn't explored, and consumables that don't add to the pile of stuff. We're avoiding personalised mugs and novelty socks—this is about thoughtful, not just thoughtful-adjacent.

You'll also find our "Off the Beaten Path" section at the end, for times when the best gift isn't a thing at all.
